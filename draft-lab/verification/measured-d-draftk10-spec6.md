## Caveats recorded with this evidence
- acceptance_rate = accepted_tokens / draft_tokens; its denominator is ~K, so 34% at K=6 and 17% at
  K=12 are NOT directly comparable. The robust statement is: same tokens/step (3.04 vs 3.04) at half
  the average draft depth (6 vs 12) -> about half the drafter forwards per emitted token.
- m0-reference ran the FULL suite, d-draftk10-spec6 the QUICK suite; tok/s numbers are indicative,
  spec-decode ratios and boot times are comparable.
- To isolate the 10-expert routing effect, a same-K control is required: d-draftk5-spec6 (K=6, folder
  top-k 5) or draft-lab/recipes/curve-k6.yaml (self-draft K=6).
